<h2>v1.0.1</h2>

<p>Localization fix: removed leftover references to the origin mod's name in advancement translation keys, and filled in translation keys that were entirely missing.</p>

<h3>Fix</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Advancements</strong>: the progression and challenge gate advancements (Haven, Frontier, Ascent, Summit, Pinnacle and their gateways) pointed their <code>translate</code> keys at the origin mod's namespace instead of <code>ascendant_equipment</code>, so titles/descriptions fell back to raw untranslated text in the advancement popup and screen.</li>
<li><strong>Advancement text</strong>: added the missing title, description and per-criterion text for all 6 progression advancements and 5 challenge gates (these keys had never been written, not just misnamed).</li>
<li><strong>World Tier screen</strong>: added the missing name/description for each world tier (Haven, Frontier, Ascent, Summit, Pinnacle) and the title/description text for all 6 stages of the World Tier tutorial, which were previously showing raw lang keys instead of readable text.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Notes</h3>
<blockquote>Shipped locales are now en_us and es_es only. The other 10 locales never had the keys above translated either; they are deferred to a future localization pass.</blockquote>
